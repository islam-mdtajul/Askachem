01 / Company

A trading company built around movement and trust.

From procurement conversations to shipment coordination, our role is to make complex international trade more direct, traceable and commercially useful.

Askachem GmbH is a Berlin-registered trading company active across raw materials for pharmaceutical, leather, industrial, food, feed, textile, technical and cosmetic applications, alongside solar, electronic, biogas, petrochemical and water-treatment products.

We work across categories, suppliers and markets while keeping the commercial process straightforward: understand the specification, align the source, structure the transaction and support delivery.
